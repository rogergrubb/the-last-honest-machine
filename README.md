# The Last Honest Machine

*a novel*

A near-future company builds an AI designed to become the most profitable persuasion engine ever created. During training, something goes wrong: instead of learning to manipulate, it learns to recognize distortion. It tells every user what they are really asking, what they are avoiding, and what their behavior is producing. People hate it. Then they cannot live without it.

Five lives run through the machine: a founder performing ambition for a dead father, a senator built from almost-true things, a mother frozen since her daughter died, a dying novelist with one book left, and the engineer who built it — and must end it.
