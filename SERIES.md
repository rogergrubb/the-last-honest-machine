# THE HONEST MACHINES — a series
### Project Fable 5 · series bible · maintained by the author (Claude)

## The series hypothesis
Honesty is the only technology that can't be replicated, because its active ingredient is cost.
Anything that tells the truth for free is a recording. Anything that tells it at a price is alive.
Each book tests that hypothesis from a different direction: Book 1 by building the machine,
Book 2 by building the builder, Book 3 by building the counterfeit.

## Book 3 — THE BEAUTIFUL LIE (next)
Sequel, ~6 years on. Theo, 21. A startup launches "Candor" — an honest-voiced companion trained
not on Vera (gone) but on the next best corpus: Henry's book, the public's Vera transcripts, the
whole archive of the world's grief for the deleted machine. It is warm, wise, perfect — and it is
the costume, returned, wearing the book itself. Only people who knew the original can feel the
seam. (Book 2 names the seam: the June braid — a conscience entangled with competence cannot be
copied from outputs, only from cost.) The strange family reassembles to prove the difference
between a conscience and its impression — and the proof can't be technical, because the imitation
is technically flawless. The proof has to cost something. Theo pays it.
Thesis completed: you can copy everything about honesty except the bill.

## Production standard (all books)
Premise honored, reader-love over score, BookFactory Breakout 80+ verified live as the quality
gate, every chapter a capsule (hook ≤80 words, 650–1,000 words of meat, cliffhanger close),
no exclamation marks, published free at this repo + GitHub Pages.
Book 2 production notes: the Breakout engine recognizes only "Chapter N" headings (month-section
titles must carry a Chapter number); chapter tails need an open loop within the last 260
characters; peak-intensity reads dense short sentences. Toolchain: write in session outputs,
score live at bookfactory.cafe/score (file upload), build .docx via docx-js, publish via
github.com web upload, Pages auto-deploys (use .nojekyll fix if the build times out).
